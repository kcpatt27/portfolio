Case study · No. 02

SQWease

Live · UsableJavaScript · HTML · CSS

A zero-dependency language-learning app built on comprehensible input, spaced repetition and a minimum effective dose. Five languages, roughly 11,500 words, from a reproducible data pipeline.

The problem

Most study tools are heavy, account-driven, or built around a single language. SQWease is a small static app: open it, study a few cards, and stop. The constraint is the feature.

Constraints

  • No framework and no runtime dependencies.
  • Multiple languages and roughly 11,500 words without hand-maintaining every page.
  • Accessibility and keyboard use are part of the product, not a later pass.

Decisions

  • Static app, generated content. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with vocabulary produced by a reproducible data pipeline.
  • Two study modes. Flashcards for recall and 1-2-3 boxes for conversation practice, plus Anki-compatible CSV export.
  • Minimum effective dose. Short sessions, keyboard shortcuts, and progress tracking instead of a large course shell.

Evidence

Lighthouse accessibility scored 1.0 on all six pages. The app ships five languages and roughly 11,500 words, and remains live and usable while it grows.

Limits

There is no account or cross-device sync. The app is still growing, which is why the status is Live · Usable rather than completed.
